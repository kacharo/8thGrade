💡 Big Idea: Followers of Jesus formed churches. Apostles like Peter and Paul spread the message across the Mediterranean.

1📖 The Story in Small BitesTap 🔊 to listen

🏠

The First Churches

People who accepted Jesus' teachings were called Christians. They formed churches — groups for worship and teaching — and met in people's homes. Many of those homes were owned by women.

🎣

Peter the Fisher

Peter was a Jewish fisher and one of Jesus' first followers. Christian tradition says he went to Rome and helped start a church there.

💡

Paul on the Road

Paul at first hated Christians. On the road to Damascus, he said he saw a bright light and heard Jesus' voice. He became a Christian on the spot!

🗺️

Paul Travels Far

Paul traveled for about 35 years, started churches around the eastern Mediterranean, and wrote letters called epistles. He spread the message to non-Jews, called Gentiles.

☁️

What Christians Believe

Christians believe Jesus is the Son of God. By accepting him, people could gain salvation — being saved from sin and going to heaven. Most Christians believe in the Trinity: one God in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
