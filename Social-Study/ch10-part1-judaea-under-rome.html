Judaea Under Roman Rule

💡 Big Idea: When Rome ruled Judaea, some Jews opposed Rome peacefully and others rebelled. After the revolts, Rome forced Jews out of Jerusalem.

1📖 The Story in Small BitesTap 🔊 to listen

👑

A Kingdom Splits

Long ago, kings David and Solomon united the Israelites. Their capital was Jerusalem. Later the kingdom split into Israel and Judah. The people of Judah were called Jews.

🦅

Rome Takes Over

In 63 B.C., Rome took over Judah. In A.D. 6, Emperor Augustus made it a Roman province called Judaea. A Roman governor ruled it instead of a king.

🤔

Different Choices

Jews disagreed about Rome. Some worked with the Romans. Some followed Jewish traditions very closely. Others moved to faraway places and shared everything.

⚔️

The Zealots Rebel

A group called the Zealots wanted to fight Rome. In A.D. 66, many Jews rebelled. Rome crushed the revolt, destroyed the temple, and killed thousands.

🧳

Forced to Leave

After another revolt in A.D. 132, Rome forced all Jews to leave Jerusalem and never return. Jews built new communities from Spain to Central Asia and kept their faith alive by studying their religious laws.
