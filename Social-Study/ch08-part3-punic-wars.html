💡 Big Idea: Rome fought three wars against Carthage, destroyed it, and took control of the whole Mediterranean Sea.

1📖 The Story in Small BitesTap 🔊 to listen

⛵

A Big Rival

Carthage was a rich trading city in North Africa. Rome and Carthage both wanted to control trade on the Mediterranean Sea, especially around Sicily.

⚔️

First Punic War (264 B.C.)

Rome built a navy for the first time and beat Carthage. Rome took Sicily. The wars are called 'Punic' from the Latin word for the people of Carthage.

🐘

Hannibal Crosses the Alps

In the Second Punic War, Carthage's general Hannibal surprised Rome. He marched about 46,000 soldiers, horses, and 37 elephants over the snowy Alps into Italy!

💥

Battle of Cannae

At Cannae (216 B.C.) in southern Italy, Hannibal crushed the Roman army. But Rome did not give up. It built new armies.

🏆

Scipio Wins at Zama

The Roman general Scipio attacked Carthage itself. Hannibal rushed home. At the Battle of Zama (202 B.C.), Scipio defeated Hannibal.

🔥

Carthage Destroyed (146 B.C.)

In the Third Punic War, Rome burned Carthage and enslaved 50,000 people. That same year, Rome also took over Greece. Romans called the Mediterranean 'our sea.'
