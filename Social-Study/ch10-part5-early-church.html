💡 Big Idea: Early Christians organized the church like the Roman Empire, with levels of leaders. They collected writings into the New Testament.

1📖 The Story in Small BitesTap 🔊 to listen

🔺

Organized Like Rome

The church copied the Roman Empire and set up a hierarchy — different levels of leaders. The clergy were church leaders. The laity were regular church members.

🪜

Levels of Leaders

Priests led local churches. A group of churches formed a diocese, led by a bishop. City bishops could become archbishops. The five leading archbishops were called patriarchs.

🤝

Deciding Beliefs

Bishops met to discuss questions about faith. Their decisions became doctrine — official church teaching.

📰

The New Testament

Four accounts of Jesus' life, by Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John, are called gospels, which means 'good news.' They were combined with Paul's letters to form the New Testament of the Bible.

✍️

Augustine

Church Fathers wrote books explaining Christianity. Augustine, a bishop in North Africa, wrote The City of God and Confessions, the story of his own journey to faith.

🔑

The Pope

The bishop of Rome claimed the authority of Peter. By A.D. 600, he was called the pope, from a word meaning 'father.' Latin-speaking churches became the Roman Catholic Church.
