1📖 The Story in Small BitesTap 🔊 to listen

3️⃣

The Second Triumvirate

After Caesar died, Octavian (Caesar's grandnephew), Antony, and Lepidus formed the Second Triumvirate. Soon Octavian and Antony became rivals.

🗣️

Cicero Speaks Up

Cicero was a famous speaker who wanted to save the republic. He gave speeches against Antony. Antony had him killed.

🚢

Battle of Actium

Antony teamed up with Cleopatra, queen of Egypt. In 31 B.C., at the Battle of Actium near Greece, Octavian crushed their army and navy.

👑

Rome's First Emperor

In 27 B.C., Octavian became the first Roman emperor. He took the title Augustus, which means 'revered (honored) one.' The republic was over — Rome was now an empire.

🕊️

The Pax Romana Begins

Augustus began about 200 years of peace called the Pax Romana ('Roman Peace').

🛠️

What Augustus Did

He built a professional army of about 150,000 citizens and a special guard of 9,000 men called the Praetorian Guard. He rebuilt Rome with marble buildings, gave grain to the poor, and made taxes fairer.
