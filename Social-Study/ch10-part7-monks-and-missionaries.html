💡 Big Idea: Monks and nuns built communities and helped people. Missionaries spread Christianity across Europe.

1📖 The Story in Small BitesTap 🔊 to listen

🏰

Monasteries

In the A.D. 300s, men called monks began living together in monasteries, away from the world, to pray and do good. Women called nuns lived in convents.

📋

Rules to Live By

Basil wrote rules for monks in the East (the Basilian Rule). Benedict wrote rules for monks in the West (the Benedictine Rule). Monks gave up belongings and spent their days in work and prayer.

🏥

Helping Others

Monks and nuns ran hospitals and schools, helped the poor, and copied old Greek and Roman books so they weren't lost. Many also served as missionaries.

🔤

Cyril's Alphabet

Brothers Cyril and Methodius took Christianity to the Slavs of Eastern Europe. About A.D. 863, Cyril invented the Cyrillic alphabet so people could read the Bible in their own language.

☘️

Patrick in Ireland

In the A.D. 400s, a priest named Patrick brought Christianity to Ireland and built monasteries and churches.

🏴

England Converts

Angles and Saxons had invaded Britain. In A.D. 597, Pope Gregory I sent about 40 monks to England. They converted King Ethelbert of Kent. In about 100 years, most of England was Christian.
