💡 Big Idea: Christianity grew even when Rome persecuted Christians. Later, emperors accepted it, and it became Rome's official religion.

1📖 The Story in Small BitesTap 🔊 to listen

🛣️

Rome Helped It Spread

Peace, safe Roman roads, and common languages (Latin and Greek) let Christians travel and share their message.

🤗

Why People Joined

Christianity gave hope to the poor and powerless. It promised happiness after death. Churches cared for the sick, the elderly, widows, and orphans. Women found new roles, too.

🚫

Rome Fights Back

Romans expected everyone to honor the emperor as a god. Christians refused. In A.D. 64, Emperor Nero blamed Christians for a great fire. Rome began to persecute them. Many became martyrs.

🕯️

The Catacombs

Christians buried their dead in catacombs — dark tunnels under Rome. More than 5 million bodies were buried there.

✝️

Constantine's Vision

In A.D. 312, Emperor Constantine said he saw a flaming cross in the sky before a battle. He won and accepted Christianity. In A.D. 313, his Edict of Milan made Christianity legal. His mother Helena helped him build churches.

📜

The Official Religion

In A.D. 392, Emperor Theodosius made Christianity Rome's official religion and banned other religions.
