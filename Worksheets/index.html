Mouse Genetics: One Trait

1. Topic Overview

Heredity is how traits pass from parents to babies[cite: 6]. A trait is a feature, like fur color[cite: 6]. Traits are controlled by genes, which are pieces of DNA[cite: 6]. A gene can have different versions called alleles[cite: 6]. A baby gets one allele from each parent[cite: 6]. In mice, the fur color gene has two alleles: F (black fur) and f (white fur)[cite: 6]. Black is dominant, and white is recessive[cite: 6]. Genotype = allele letters (like Ff); Phenotype = what you see (like black fur)[cite: 6].
